Bluetooth setup and diagnostics

Browsers only let a page use Bluetooth services it names in advance, and the BlueDOT's service UUID is not published. Find it once and paste it here:

  1. In Chrome or Edge open chrome://bluetooth-internals/#devices and press Start Scan.
  2. Find BlueDOT and press Inspect.
  3. Copy everything under Services (or the whole page) and paste it below. Extra text is ignored.

The free nRF Connect phone app shows the same list. Or run python3 tools/discover.py from this project.
